  • Privacy

    • Last Updated: May 14, 2026

    At Dappled Root Atelier, accessible from dappledroot.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Dappled Root Atelier and how we use it.

    1. Information We Collect

    If you reach out to us via our contact form or email, we may receive personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and the contents of the message you send us. We only collect information that is necessary to provide you with our gardening and design services.

    2. How We Use Your Information

    We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

    • Provide, operate, and maintain our website and services.
    • Improve, personalize, and expand our garden design offerings.
    • Understand and analyze how you use our website.
    • Communicate with you regarding project inquiries and appointments.
    • Send you emails related to your specific garden design project.

    3. Log Files

    Dappled Root Atelier follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

    4. Cookies and Web Beacons

    Like any other website, Dappled Root Atelier uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content.

    5. Data Security

    We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable.

    6. Contact Us

    If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at hello@dappledroot.com.
