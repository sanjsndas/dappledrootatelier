    • Our Roots

    Founded in the heart of Portland, Dappled Root Atelier began as a small collection of heirloom seeds and a notebook full of sketches. We recognized that in an increasingly digital world, the visceral connection to soil, scent, and season was being lost.

    Our mission is to bridge that gap, providing the expertise and artistic vision needed to transform any outdoor space into a sanctuary of peace and biodiversity.

    Sustainable Craft

    We prioritize native species and organic practices to ensure that your garden supports local pollinators and the broader ecosystem.

    Living Art

    Every trellis, path, and planting bed is designed with an artist's eye, focusing on the interplay of light and shadow—the "dappled" effect that gives us our name.
